Sino Sila?

Sino Sila? is an independent, unofficial project. House rosters come from Wikipedia and are being cross-checked; House portraits are from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each profile. Data comes from the Senate of the Philippines' public Legislative Information System and the sources linked on each item. It records public facts and is not an accusation of wrongdoing. Counts show volume, not quality or importance. Data as of October 1, 2026.
