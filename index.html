Sino Sila? is an independent project, not affiliated with the Senate or the House. Every fact links to the public source it came from. Senate data comes from the Senate's Legislative Information System. House rosters come from Wikipedia and are still being checked against other sources. Portraits come from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each profile. School seals come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons and belong to their institutions.

Court cases come from news and court records, one person at a time. “Not yet checked” means we have not confirmed a record from two independent sources, which is not the same as a clean record. A case listed here is a public record, not a finding of guilt. If you spot a mistake or something out of date, we want to fix it. Membership data as of October 1, 2026; court cases as of November 13, 2026.
